---
title: "bt_radial"
description: "Viewport-responsive radial for equipped weapons, phone and inventory."
icon: "circle-dot"
---

<Warning>Version `0.1.0` is a pre-release.</Warning>

Hold the configured key, move away from the neutral center to select a tile,
click to confirm, or release to close without applying. Equipped weapons come
from the official Open77 weapon snapshot.

Dependencies: `bt_core >=0.8.1`, `open77_weapons >=0.1.0`,
`bt_phone >=0.1.0`, and `bt_inventory >=0.1.0`.

The surface inherits the player's viewport. The wheel scale and dead zone are
recomputed from `innerWidth` and `innerHeight`; it remains centered rather
than adopting the left-side optical-window layout.
