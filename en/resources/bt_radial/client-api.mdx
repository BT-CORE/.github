---
title: "bt_radial client behavior"
description: "Lifecycle and cross-resource behavior."
icon: "display"
---

`bt_radial` publishes no public client export in `0.1.0`.

At open time it calls `bt_core.isReady` through the client export bridge. The
phone and inventory tiles call `bt_phone.open` and `bt_inventory.toggle`
through the same bridge; local Lua events are not used as a cross-resource bus.

The radial temporarily blocks `Attack` and `WeaponWheel`, releases both on
close/resource stop, and reads equipped slots through
`Open77.weapons.snapshot()`.
