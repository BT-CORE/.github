---
title: "Configure bt_radial"
description: "Hold key and neutral-zone settings."
icon: "sliders"
---

`shared/config.lua` exposes:

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Config.openKey` | `"CTRL"` | Rebindable hold key; empty disables mapping. |
| `Config.deadZone` | `72` | Neutral radius on the 1920×1080 reference surface. |

The dead zone is a design reference, not a fixed screen assumption: the WebUI
scales it with the actual viewport.
