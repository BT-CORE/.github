---
title: "bt_lib client modules"
description: "Raycasts, entities, vectors, character position, formatting and WebUI lifecycle."
icon: "display"
---

`require("@bt_lib")` returns:

- `raycast`: cast, clearance, oriented footprint, ground classification,
  screen picking and best-heading helpers;
- `entities`: nearby/nearest queries and traffic-light helpers;
- `vectors`: 2D/3D distances, bearings, yaw and forward offsets;
- `character.position()`;
- `format.eddies(amount)`;
- `webui`: create/get/readiness/send/focus/destroy.

`webui.setFocus(keyboard, cursor)` follows the official Open77 argument order.
`setCursorFocus(want)` is shorthand for `setFocus(false, want)`.

The importing resource must declare every permission required by the native
calls it makes through these modules.
